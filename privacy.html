Privacy Policy

I respect your privacy. Here’s what you need to know.

This Privacy Policy applies to afstevens.com and signalandclarity.com. It explains how Drew Stevens collects, uses and protects your personal information.

What Information Do I Collect?

If I Send You a Cold Email

  • Your name, if publicly available
  • Your business or work email address

If You Contact Me

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Any information you choose to share in your message

How I Use Your Information

Cold Email Outreach
I may use your business email address to contact you about services that I believe may be relevant to your business. If your name is publicly available, I may use it to personalise the message.

Contact Form Enquiries
If you contact me through the website, I use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about that enquiry.

I do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing

I rely on legitimate interest when contacting professionals or businesses about services that may be relevant to them. You can object to this processing at any time.

When you contact me directly, I process the information you provide so I can respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, take steps connected with a potential working relationship.

Third-Party Service Providers

I use a small number of third-party services to operate the business and website, including:

  • Microsoft Outlook for email
  • Netlify for website hosting and contact-form submissions

These providers may process personal information on my behalf in order to provide their services. I do not sell or share your data for their own advertising purposes.

Data Retention

I retain contact information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, unless a longer period is required for legal, accounting or administrative reasons.

Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to certain uses of your personal data, including direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing

How to Exercise Your Rights

To make a request, you can:

  • Reply to an email from me
  • Use the website contact form

I’ll respond within the time required by applicable data-protection law.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have a privacy question, you can reply to an email from me or use the website contact form.

Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this website.
